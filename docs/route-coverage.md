## Cross-page acceptance

Five offers remain distinct. Reg E and Reg Z are separate. Investigator finding and notice approval are explicit. Provisional credit proposal, approval and synthetic completion differ. Unsupported inputs invalidate Reg E dates and dependent approvals. Missing evidence and source versions remain visible. No send, fund movement, filing or bank write exists. No customer logos, testimonials, certification badges, live integration claims or ROI counters were invented. Prices remain hypotheses; a local draft never counts as a paid pilot or qualified demand.

Production launch dependencies remain: verified controller/contact/deletion route, counsel-approved rules and legal text, real consented form delivery and retention/spam controls, authorized analytics receipt and institutionally approved authentication if needed. A live pilot additionally requires sandbox/access, privacy/model risk, representative replay with zero critical errors, compliance sign-off and paid supervised commitment.
